# Business Analysis Presentation

## MavenTech CRM Sales Operations Analytics

This presentation extends the Power BI dashboard analysis into a management-focused business analysis of CRM sales performance.

The presentation examines key performance findings, potential business risks, and areas requiring deeper investigation, then translates those findings into recommendations and corrective actions to support data-driven decision-making.

### Analysis Focus

- Executive sales performance
- Revenue and product performance
- Regional and sales-team performance
- Performance gaps and potential business risks
- Root-cause hypotheses and diagnostic analysis
- SWOT analysis
- Management recommendations and corrective actions
- Continuous improvement and decision support

### Management Framework

The analysis follows a continuous improvement approach:

**Monitor → Diagnose → Investigate → Act**

This framework helps management move beyond historical reporting by using dashboard evidence to identify performance differences, investigate potential drivers, and determine targeted actions.

### Presentation Purpose

The Power BI dashboard provides the interactive analytical foundation, while this presentation focuses on interpreting the results from a management perspective and translating analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.
